About

From physical craft to digital experience.

My design journey started at the Indian Institute of Craft and Design, where I learned to see form, function, materials, and people as one connected system.

I began by developing physical products with artisan communities—researching root causes, building sustainable solutions, training makers, and learning through feedback. That experience shaped the way I work today: get close to the real problem, understand the people living with it, and make the solution useful before making it impressive.

I later moved into digital experience design, working across UX research, information architecture, wireframes, prototypes, usability testing, and interface design. At Uxmint, I contributed to e-commerce experiences for Alshaya Group brands including H&M, Victoria’s Secret, American Eagle, West Elm, and Pottery Barn. Today, I design at Optiblack while continuing to explore AI-assisted product workflows.
